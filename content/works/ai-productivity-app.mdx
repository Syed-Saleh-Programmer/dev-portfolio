# FlowState — Frictionless Brain Capture

## The Vision
Creative developers and thinkers often lose their best breakthroughs because existing note apps require manual categorization, tagging, and folder structures. FlowState eliminates all capture friction: talk or brain-dump, and the system synthesizes priorities automatically.

## Engineering Highlights
- **Cross-Platform Flutter & Dart:** 60fps gesture interactions, silky micro-animations, and responsive desktop/mobile layout sharing 95% of business logic.
- **Local-First Synchronization:** Employs an offline-first SQLite cache that reconciles with a remote PostgreSQL instance over optimistic conflict-free replicated data types.
- **Semantic Note Interconnection:** Automatically creates bi-directional graph links between notes based on semantic cosine similarity.
