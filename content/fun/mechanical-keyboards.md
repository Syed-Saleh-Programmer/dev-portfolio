As developers, we touch the keyboard for 8 to 12 hours every day. Treating that primary input device like a commodity felt wrong.

Four years ago, I fell down the rabbit hole of custom mechanical keyboards: designing PCBs, soldering microcontrollers (RP2040), flashing QMK/ZMK firmware with custom layers, and fine-tuning switch acoustics. Building hardware with your hands connects physical physics to software triggers.
