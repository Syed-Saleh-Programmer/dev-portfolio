Photography is my way of resetting my visual perception. When writing code, everything is deterministic, logical, and composed of syntax trees.

Behind a 35mm lens, the world is chaotic, fleeting, and governed by light. I love wandering through quiet alleyways past midnight with an old mechanical rangefinder, looking for unexpected reflections, lonely diner signs, and geometric architecture. Developing physical film taught me patience — you can't hot-reload an exposure.
