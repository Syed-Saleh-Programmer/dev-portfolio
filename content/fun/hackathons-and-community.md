There is a unique adrenaline rush in assembling a team of three engineers, picking an ambitious problem from scratch, and having a working production deploy live 36 hours later.

I actively participate in university and regional hackathons, both as a builder and a mentor for junior developers. These high-pressure sprints taught me how to triage technical trade-offs, decouple mission-critical paths, and build intuitive user interfaces under tight constraints.
